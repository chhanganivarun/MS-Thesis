\paragraph{Author contribution statement.}
The ICRA~2023 paper above is the archival publication corresponding to this thesis. Kanishk Jain and Varun Chhangani are listed as equal first authors on that paper. For the work reported here, the candidate's contributions include: (i)~design and implementation of the CARLA-NAV data-collection toolkit and a substantial share of episode annotation in the observer, navigator, and verifier roles; (ii)~development of the live-navigation pipeline that converts predicted navigable-region masks into planner targets and applies the area-based stopping criterion; (iii)~training and evaluation of the CLIP-based multi-frame variants and ablations reported in Chapter~\ref{ch:vln}; and (iv)~analysis of failure modes and evaluation limitations. Co-authors contributed complementary modelling components, additional annotation, and advising. Dataset curation was performed jointly by the candidate, Amogh Tiwari, and Kanishk Jain (with KV~Aditya Srivatsa assisting early tooling), with Dr.~Vineet Gandhi providing complex commands and verification in ambiguous cases; this reconciles the collection-team description in Chapter~\ref{ch:dataset} with the ICRA author list.
